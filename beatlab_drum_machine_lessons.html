🎓 Lessons

1. What is rhythm?

Rhythm is the pattern of sounds and silences. A simple beat uses four main counts:

1 — 2 — 3 — 4

  • Kick: the low “thump” that anchors the beat.
  • Snare: the sharp hit often heard on beats 2 and 4.
  • Hi-hat: short sounds that keep time.

2. Your first beat

Try putting Kick on steps 1, 5, 9, 13, Snare on 5 and 13, and closed hats on every other step.

3. Learn by listening

Start slowly around 80–100 BPM. Once the pattern feels comfortable, raise the tempo gradually.
