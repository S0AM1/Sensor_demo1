🎓 Lessons

1. Rhythm

Most basic beats count 1, 2, 3, 4. The kick provides the low pulse, while the snare often lands on 2 and 4.

2. Your First Beat

  • Kick: steps 1, 5, 9, 13
  • Snare: steps 5 and 13
  • Closed hi-hat: every other step

3. Practice

Start slowly, listen to the timing, then gradually increase the tempo.
